Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account details: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To display your expense history and summaries.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed within 30 days. We recommend you export any records you wish to keep before deleting your account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting or database services) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. You may do this directly from your account settings or by contacting us.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the app's contact page. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
